The useful question is not whether every loss can be eliminated. It is which losses were preventable—and when enough information first existed to act.
Signals worth investigating

Five places earned margin can become exposed.

Change exposure

Extra work is performed before notice, evidence, pricing and authorization are under control.

Late labor visibility

Forecast deterioration appears only after a large share of the available hours are consumed.

Billing delay

Completed work remains unapproved, unsupported or not ready to bill.

Weak accountability

Known project issues do not become owned actions with escalation and closure.

Management overload

Owners become the default escalation point because too many exceptions must be manually reconstructed.
