Where does earned revenue get stranded?

A project can look profitable while the contractor still finances the work because changes, documentation, approvals and billing are moving more slowly than field execution.

Profit protected, loss avoided, billing accelerated and cash released are different economic outcomes. A useful review keeps them separate.
Follow value until it stops moving

Identified → documented → noticed → priced → submitted → approved → billed → collected.

The review asks where value stalled, who owned the next action, what evidence was missing, and which portion of the delay was internally preventable.

Unapproved change exposure

Quantify work that is still waiting on pricing, submission, negotiation or approval.

Billing readiness

Find completed work that cannot yet be billed because supporting requirements are incomplete.

Rejected applications

Identify recurring rejection reasons that can be corrected upstream.

Evidence weakness

Determine whether disputed changes and backcharges have the chronology and contemporaneous support needed.

Customer vs. internal delay

Separate customer payment behavior from preventable documentation, approval and process failures.
